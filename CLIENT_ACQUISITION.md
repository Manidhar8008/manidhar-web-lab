## 🎬 Adding Client Work Strategically

### Attract More Clients With Social Proof

When you finish a client project:

1. **Get permission** to showcase it (add NDA term to contracts)
2. **Add to `/projects/` with `type: "client"`
3. **Set `featured: true` in meta.json**
4. **Screenshot + live URL**
5. **Project appears in README** under "🤝 Client Work"

This signals: "Real companies hire me. You should too."

### What to Showcase

- ✅ **SaaS companies** (looks premium)
- ✅ **Funded startups** (social proof)
- ✅ **B2B tools** (shows scope)
- ✅ **Financial products** (trust signal)
- ✅ **Consumer apps** (show scale)

❌ Avoid: Small projects, personal blogs, internal tools
